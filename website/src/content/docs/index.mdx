<HomeTop>
blackhole-threats reads threat feeds from files and URLs, pulls out the IPv4 and IPv6 prefixes,
summarises them and announces them to your BGP peers with a community such as `64520:666`. Your
routers match the community and drop the traffic. When a feed fails, its routes stay as they were.
